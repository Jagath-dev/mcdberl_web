# McD BERL Design Context

## Surface

Marketing homepage / brand site. The visitor should understand McD BERL's point of view, see proof through selected work and client voices, and find a path to connect or join the team.

## Visual world

- Warm paper white `#f8f8f6`, ink `#1d1d1b`, hairline `#d9d8d3`, and McD red `#c83d32`.
- Work Sans is the utility/body face; Jost is reserved for the hero; Georgia gives the editorial serif moments.
- The signature is the red vertical rule paired with an oversized serif statement.
- Photography is dark, documentary, and people/places-first. Overlays should stay quiet and legible.

## Behavior

- Navigation is a sticky desktop bar and an accessible collapsible mobile menu.
- Project and testimonial carousels are button-controlled, keyboard reachable, and preserve clear current/total counts.
- All meaningful source links remain real anchors; visual sections use stable IDs for navigation and SEO.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain visible focus rings.
